How to use Hemi-Lab

Before you start

  • Wear headphones. Binaural beats need one tone per ear.
  • Find a quiet, comfortable place to lie back.
  • Set the volume low. It should be barely there.
  • Set an intention for the session.

The Focus levels

The presets borrow the Monroe Institute's map: 10 body asleep and mind awake, 12 expanded awareness, 15 no-time, 21 the bridge to other energy systems, and 23+ the contact states. Start at 10 and work your way up over many sessions.

After

  • Lie still for a minute before you sit up.
  • Journal right away, because details fade fast.
  • Check Patterns now and then to see which levels and times of day work best for you.

Please don't use it if…

…you're driving or operating anything, or you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder (talk to your doctor first). If you feel dizzy or uneasy, end the session, take off the headphones and breathe.
